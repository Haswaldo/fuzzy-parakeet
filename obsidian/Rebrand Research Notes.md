## Task 1: Context
**Rebrand examples:** Innocent (1999), Instagram (2016), Twitter → X (2023)

**Timeline**
- 1876: first UK registered trade mark (Bass Brewery red triangle)
- 1907: first corporate identity (Peter Behrens for AEG)
- 1971: Nike Swoosh (Carolyn Davidson, paid $35)
- 1977: Apple rainbow logo (Rob Janoff)
- 2010: Gap logo U-turn (Laird + Partners)
- 2023: Twitter becomes X

**Brand anatomy:** Name (Innocent), Logo (Nike), Colour (Spotify), Typography (Coca-Cola), Imagery (Apple), Tone (Innocent), Values (Nike), Positioning (Apple)

**Evolution case studies:** Lucozade (sick-bed drink → sports drink), Burger King (1999 → 2021 by Jones Knowles Ritchie), Greggs (product/marketing shift, vegan sausage roll 2019)

## Task 2: Failures
| | Gap 2010 | Tropicana 2009 | Consignia 2001 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Changed | Blue box → Helvetica + gradient square | Orange + straw → plain glass (Arnell Group) | Post Office Group → "Consignia" |
| Why | Look modern | Look premium | Look global |
| Reaction | Social media backlash | Shoppers couldn't find it | Press ridicule |
| Cost | Reputation | ~20% sales drop, ~$30m | ~£2m + trust |
| Recovery | Reversed in ~1 week | ~2 months | Royal Mail Group in 2002 |
| Lesson | Don't drop an icon overnight | Keep the shelf cue | Heritage is an asset |

## Task 2: Successes
| | Mastercard 2016 | Burberry 2023 | Airbnb 2014 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Changed | Simplified circles, lowercase (Pentagram); name dropped 2019 | Archive knight logo + serif wordmark | Bélo symbol + coral (DesignStudio) |
| Why | Work on phones/contactless | Reconnect with British heritage | "Belong Anywhere" |
| Kept | The circles | Knight, name, check | Name, friendly feel |
| Tested/explained | 80%+ recognised circles alone | Heritage campaigns | Launch film + story |
| Steal this | Simplify, keep the shape | Use the archive | Give the logo a story |

**My 3 rules:** keep the asset people recognise · test with real customers first · tell the story before the reveal

## Brand equity (assets audiences value)
- **Logo:** Gap lost 20+ years of recognition in a week
- **Colour:** Cadbury purple, defended in court
- **Packaging:** Tropicana carton
- **Mascot:** Duracell bunny
- **Sonic:** McDonald's "I'm lovin' it"

## Audience targeting
- **Demographic:** age/gender/income (Boots No7, women 25+)
- **Geographic:** place (McDonald's menus by country)
- **Psychographic:** values/lifestyle (Patagonia, eco buyers)
- **Behavioural:** habits/loyalty (Tesco Clubcard)
- **Persona:** research-based profile of one person who stands for many
- **Primary research** = data you collect · **Secondary** = data others made

## Testing methods
| Method | How | Example | + | − |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focus groups | 6–10 customers discuss with a host | New Coke 1985 | Deep views | Tiny sample |
| A/B testing | Two versions, measure the winner | Netflix cover art | Real data | Costly |
| Social listening | Track posts about a brand | Gap 2010 | Instant | Loud few |
| Soft launch | Release in one place first | Instagram Reels, Brazil | Low risk | Can leak |
