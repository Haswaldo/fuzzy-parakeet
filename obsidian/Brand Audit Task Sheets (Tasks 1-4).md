# Brand Audit Task Sheets (Tasks 1–4)

Part of [[Rebrand Project - Progress Hub]]. Brief: [[Rebranding Project Brief]]. Research content: [[Rebrand Research Notes]].

> These four sheets are a separate set from the deck's "Task 1/2/3" numbering. Here: **Task 1 = brand audit, 2 = SWOT, 3 = audience profile, 4 = competitor analysis.** All work is on **one chosen brand**.

---

## Task 1: Brand Audit Prompt Questions
*Answer each prompt for your chosen brand. Use screenshots and examples as evidence.*

### Brand Kit
**Task: Design the brand kit for the brand.**
1. What do the logo, colours and fonts say about the brand?
2. Describe the tone of voice in three adjectives, with an example.
3. What are the brand's values, and who is the target audience?
4. **Evaluate:** Does the visual identity match the brand's values?

### Packaging
1. What materials, shapes and colours are used, and why?
2. What do you notice first, second and third on the pack?
3. Are any sustainability claims convincing? What is the evidence?
4. **Evaluate:** How does it compare with a competitor on the shelf?

### Website
1. What are your first impressions within five seconds?
2. How many clicks to buy? Does it work well on mobile?
3. Is the brand kit used consistently? Is the site accessible?
4. **Evaluate:** Biggest strength, biggest weakness, one improvement?

### Social Media
1. Which platforms are used, how often, and what types of content?
2. How does the brand reply to comments, even negative ones?
3. Are influencer partnerships clearly labelled, for example #ad?
4. **Evaluate:** Which platform works best, and which is underused?

**Pull it together:** SWOT analysis | score each area out of 5 for consistency | three justified recommendations

## Task 2: SWOT Analysis
*Use your brand audit findings as evidence. Strengths and weaknesses are **internal**; opportunities and threats are **external**.*

### Strengths (internal)
1. Which area of your audit scored highest, and why?
2. What does the brand do better than its competitors?
3. What do customers praise in reviews and comments?
**Evidence:** back up each point with a screenshot, statistic or quote.

### Weaknesses (internal)
1. Which area of your audit scored lowest, and why?
2. Where is the branding inconsistent across channels?
3. What do customers criticise or complain about?
**Evidence:** back up each point with a screenshot, statistic or quote.

### Opportunities (external)
1. Which trends could the brand use, such as sustainability?
2. Are there new platforms, audiences or markets to reach?
3. Could a collaboration or partnership help the brand grow?
**Evidence:** back up each point with a screenshot, statistic or quote.

### Threats (external)
1. Which competitors are gaining ground, and how?
2. How could changing habits or the cost of living affect sales?
3. Could bad publicity or new regulations harm the brand?
**Evidence:** back up each point with a screenshot, statistic or quote.

**Then:** write three justified recommendations, each linked to a weakness or an opportunity from your SWOT.

## Task 3: Audience Profile
*Who is your brand really talking to? Research the audience, then bring them to life as a persona.*

### Build a Persona
- Name and an illustrated or stock image
- Age, location and occupation
- Interests and hobbies
- Values and beliefs
- Favourite platforms and influencers
- Frustrations and pain points
- A quote they might say about the brand

**Tip:** describe a typical day. When do they see, buy and use the product?

### Demographics
1. Who is the core customer: age, location, income?
2. Is there a secondary audience?

### Psychographics
1. What do they value and care about?
2. What lifestyle do they aspire to?

### Media Habits
1. Which platforms and creators do they follow?
2. Where do they meet the brand?

### Buying Behaviour
1. Why, when and how often do they buy?
2. What would make them switch brands?

**Evidence:** justify your profile using your audit findings, the brand's own content and published audience data.
