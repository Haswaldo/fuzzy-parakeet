# Outreach scripts

`out/outreach.csv` already fills these in per lead. Edit the wording to sound like you.

## First message (SMS / WhatsApp / DM / contact form)

> Hi, is that {name}? I noticed you don't have a website, so I built a free preview of one for you: {url}
> If you like it, I can put it live on your own domain for £295 one-off plus £25/month for hosting and edits. No obligation. If it's not for you, just ignore this and I won't message again. - {you}

## Follow-up (day 3)

> Hi {name}, just checking you saw the preview I made: {url}. Happy to change the wording or add photos if you send them. I'm only keeping it up until Friday.

Only say "until Friday" if you will actually take it down.

## Phone opener (they answer)

> Hi, is that {name}? I'm [you]. I built a free draft website for your business, and I'll text you the link. It takes 30 seconds to look at. If you like it I can make it live this week. If not, no problem.

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I get work from Facebook / word of mouth" | "Fair. This gives those people somewhere to check you out and a button to call. It's £295, once." |
| "How much?" | "£295 to put it live on your domain, then £25/month covers hosting and edits. No contract; cancel any time." |
| "I don't have a domain" | "I'll register one in your name. Roughly £10-15/year, I'll pass it on at cost." |
| "Not interested" | "No problem, thanks for looking." Then stop. |

## Before you take payment

- Confirm the name, phone, area and services with the owner in writing.
- Get a payment link first (Stripe Payment Link). Don't go live on trust.
- Put the domain and hosting in their name or document clearly who owns what.
